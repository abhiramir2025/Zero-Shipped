      How it works

      1

      Upload

      Drop in the PDF export of your resume. In the real build, the text layer gets extracted server-side.

      2

      Parse

      The parser matches resume text against the skill list for the role you picked.

      3

      Ship

      You get a score, the skills you can prove, and a punch list of what to add before you apply.

      Why bother

      🎯 Role-aware scoring

      A resume is never "good" in the abstract — it's good for a job. Scoring is always against a target role.

      🧩 Gap lists, not vibes

      Instead of a vague "looks great", you get a concrete list of missing keywords recruiters filter on.

      🔒 Browser-only demo

      This mock runs entirely client-side. Your file never leaves the page — no backend, no storage.
